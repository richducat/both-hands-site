Support

Answers to the common ones, and how to reach us.

My taps feel late (or early)

Open Tune timing on the home screen and tap along with sixteen clicks. The game measures your phone's own delay and allows for it. Bluetooth headphones add their own delay, so re-tune after switching.

How do stars work?

Each song has three stars: half of the maximum score earns one, three quarters earns two, and 92% or better earns all three. Stars across songs unlock the next ones.

What does iPhone Duo add?

Open the phone flat and each thumb owns a half. On yellow Squeeze bars, pinch the fold slightly on the beat. Fold the phone like a laptop and set it down and the lower half becomes four drum pads.

Versus mode

Two players share one phone, side by side. Both get the same pattern; the higher score wins.

Something's broken

Email richducat@gmail.com with your iPhone model and what happened. We read everything.
