Both Hands

Support

The notes feel early or late

Open Settings (the gear on the home screen) and choose Tune timing. Tap along to sixteen clicks and the game lines itself up with what you hear. Wireless headphones and speakers each add their own delay, so tune again after you switch.

A song won't add

Songs from a streaming subscription can't be added. They are copy-protected, so no app can read them. Songs you own as files work: MP3, M4A, WAV and AIFF.

The song needs to be between 20 seconds and 12 minutes long, and it needs a rhythm the game can find. Speech and free-flowing music are added too: the notes then follow the sounds, and the song is marked "No steady beat". A recording with nothing to tap along to, such as a steady hiss or a drone, is turned away.

Removing a song you added

On the home screen, touch and hold the song under Your Music and choose Remove. The game's copy is deleted. Your original file is not touched.

Playing with two people

Choose a song and tap Two players. Each player takes one side of the screen and gets the same notes.

Pads instead of lanes

Turn on Pads layout in Settings to play on four large pads along the bottom of the screen.

Which iPhones it runs on

Any iPhone with iOS 26 or later.
