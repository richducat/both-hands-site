Both Hands

Privacy

Both Hands collects nothing. There is no account, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking.

What stays on your phone

Your scores and settings. Best scores, marks, your streak and your timing setting are saved on your phone only.

Songs you add. When you add a song from your files, Both Hands copies it into its own storage on your phone and works out the beat there. The song, its title and its cover are never uploaded, shared or sent to anyone. Removing the song in the game deletes that copy.

What leaves your phone

Nothing. The game works fully offline and makes no network connections of its own. The music credits screen has links to where the built-in songs came from; they open in your browser only if you tap them.

Children

Both Hands is suitable for all ages and collects no information from anyone.

Purchases

The game is bought once through the App Store. Apple handles the payment; Both Hands never sees your payment details.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
