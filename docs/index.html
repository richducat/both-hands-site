Both Hands

One phone. Both thumbs. Perfect timing.

A rhythm game where your left thumb plays the left half and your right thumb plays the right. Built for iPhone Duo: squeeze the fold on the beat, or fold it like a laptop for drum pads.

What's inside

  • 8 original tracks, generated inside the app — no ads, no accounts, no tracking
  • Six puzzle types: Mirror, Call & Response, Stretch, Crossfire, Roll and Hinge Beat
  • Stars, daily streaks and a Versus mode for two players on one phone
  • Works on every iPhone; extra moves unlock on iPhone Duo
